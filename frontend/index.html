Speedrun Ghosts

Connect your wallet and load the level from the contract. Controls: Arrow keys / A/D to move, Space to jump.

Leaderboard (verified on-chain)

Both Ethereum Sepolia (Chain ID 11155111) and Base Sepolia (84532) are configured, each with its own contract address and RPC, and are never shared. Ethereum Sepolia is our deployment and manual-verification target; Base Sepolia is supported/configurable for a separate deployment by someone with its test ETH, but we have not deployed to or verified it. All level data (dimensions, obstacles, tick rate) is read from the selected contract at runtime - the contract is the single source of truth.
